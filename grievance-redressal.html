Grievance Redressal

We want to understand and review concerns about Joamex bookings, services, payments, or partner accounts.

How to submit a grievance

Email info@joamex.in, call +91 93917 69636, or use the Contact Us page. The contact page lists customer support hours as 10:00 AM–5:00 PM and asks customers to contact Joamex for operating-day details.

Use the subject line “Grievance” and include the following details so the team can review your concern:

  • Your name and the email address or phone number associated with your account.
  • Booking or partner account reference, if available.
  • A clear description of the issue, including relevant dates.
  • Any previous support case reference and the resolution you are requesting.

Please send only information relevant to your concern. Do not include passwords, OTPs, full payment-card numbers, or card security codes.

What happens after you contact us

  1. Joamex support reviews the details you provide and may contact you using the contact information associated with your account or message.
  2. If more information is needed, reply with the requested details and the same booking or case reference.
  3. If the issue remains unresolved, reply to the existing email thread or contact us again, mark the message as an escalation, and include the earlier case reference and any response received.

We do not publish a fixed acknowledgement or resolution deadline, or a named grievance officer, on this page. Contact Joamex using the channels above for current assistance and keep a copy of your message and any case reference.

Other support options

For booking questions that are not a formal complaint, see the Help Centre. For booking cancellations or refund requests, see the Cancellation & Refund Policy.
