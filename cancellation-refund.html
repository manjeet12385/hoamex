Cancellation & Refund Policy

Information about requesting a booking cancellation and asking Joamex to review a refund.

Request a cancellation

If you need to cancel a booking, contact Joamex as soon as possible through the Contact Us page or email info@joamex.in. Include your booking reference, registered contact details, and the service date.

Cancellation eligibility, timing, and any applicable charges can depend on the terms shown for your booking. Please review the booking details and contact support to confirm how they apply. Do not assume a booking has been cancelled until Joamex confirms its status.

If a service professional has already been assigned or the service has started, mention this when you contact support so the team can review the request against the applicable booking terms.

Request a refund review

For a payment or refund question, email info@joamex.in or use the Contact Us page. Include your booking reference, transaction date, payment method, and a short explanation of the issue and the resolution you are requesting.

Refund eligibility and amount depend on the applicable booking terms and review of the request. A refund is not guaranteed by submitting a request; Joamex will confirm the decision and any applicable amount with you.

Refund processing

If a refund is approved, Joamex will confirm the applicable amount and processing details for your booking. The time for the funds to appear may depend on the payment method and payment provider. We do not publish one fixed processing timeline for every payment method; contact support with your booking reference if you need an update.

Never share your password, OTP, full payment-card number, or card security code when contacting support.

Need further help?

Visit the Help Centre for support topics. If your concern remains unresolved, see Grievance Redressal for escalation instructions.
