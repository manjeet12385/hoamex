Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions from Joamex customers and service partners.

For customers

How do I book a home service?

Choose a service from the services page, review the details shown, and follow the booking steps on screen. Keep your booking reference for support requests.

Where can I find the price for a service?

Review the service and price details displayed during your booking before you confirm. If anything is unclear, contact support before proceeding.

How can I change or cancel a booking?

Contact Joamex as soon as possible using the Contact Us page. Share your booking reference and requested change. Any applicable cancellation terms will depend on the booking.

What should I do if I have a concern about a service?

Contact support with your booking reference and a description of the concern. For formal complaint handling or escalation, see Grievance Redressal.

How do I ask about a payment or refund?

Email info@joamex.in or use our Contact Us page. Include your booking reference and transaction date, but never share a full card number, password, or OTP.

For service partners

How can I join Joamex as a service partner?

Start from the partner registration page and follow the onboarding steps shown there.

Where can I review partner responsibilities and commission terms?

Read the Partner Terms. Commission and payout details are governed by the applicable partner agreement or account information provided to you.

Who can help with my partner account?

Contact info@joamex.in and include the email address used for registration. Do not include your password or OTP.
