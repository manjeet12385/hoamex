Partner Terms

A practical overview of the responsibilities expected when providing services through Joamex.

Accurate registration and account security

Provide complete and accurate onboarding information, including your name, email, phone number, experience, service category, skills, and any documents requested for your application. Keep your contact details current and protect your account credentials and OTPs.

Where requested in the onboarding process, provide valid supporting documents and accurate bank-account details. These details are used to review your application and support partner payments. Tell Joamex if information you submitted changes or was entered incorrectly.

Professional service and customer care

  • Accept only work you are qualified and equipped to perform.
  • Review the assigned service details and communicate clearly about scope, timing, and any material changes before proceeding.
  • Treat customers respectfully and take reasonable care of their home, belongings, and personal information.
  • Follow applicable safety requirements and raise access, safety, or service concerns with Joamex promptly.
  • Do not request or use a customer's passwords, OTPs, or payment-card security details.

Pricing, commission, and payouts

Applicable service pricing, partner commission, deductions, and payout schedules are specific to the details shared in your partner account or accepted partner agreement. Review those details before accepting work. This page does not set a commission rate, guarantee a number of bookings, or replace your individual payment terms.

For a payment question, contact info@joamex.in with your registered email and relevant booking reference. Do not send passwords, OTPs, or complete bank credentials by email.

Legal compliance and privacy

Follow applicable laws and safety requirements while delivering services. Use customer contact and address information only to coordinate and fulfil the relevant service, and do not disclose or retain it for unrelated purposes. Keep your own account credentials private.

Partner application information may include identity or qualification documents and bank details. Use the platform only to submit information requested for onboarding, and contact Joamex if you need help correcting or updating it.

Service issues and partner agreement

Report service incidents, disputes, or account concerns to Joamex promptly with the relevant booking or account details. The terms accepted during partner onboarding and any applicable platform policies govern your participation; this page is a general overview and does not replace those terms.

If any point about your application, assigned work, commission, or payout is unclear, ask Joamex for clarification before relying on it.

For help, visit the Help Centre or contact Joamex.
